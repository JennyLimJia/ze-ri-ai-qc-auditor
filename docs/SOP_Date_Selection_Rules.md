# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Date Selection (Ze Ri) Quality Control Audit Framework

## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Scope
This document outlines the standard operating rules and quality assurance framework for evaluating and auditing candidate dates for Chinese Metaphysics events (e.g., Renovation, Activation, Moving In, Business Launch).

The objective of this framework is twofold:
1. **Hard Elimination:** Identify and eliminate any candidate date that carries fatal spatial, temporal, personal, or structural afflictions.
2. **Tiered Classification:** Grade surviving safe dates into prioritized tiers (Tier 1 to Tier 3) based on energy harmony, Day Officer selection, and supporting auxiliary indicators.

---

## 2. Layer 1: Hard Elimination Rules (Automatic Rejection)

Any candidate date exhibiting one or more of the following conditions must be immediately disqualified and excluded from client recommendations.

### 2.1 Spatial & Property Afflictions
* **Sitting Mountain Three Killings (*San Sha*):** Rejection of any date where the House Sitting direction (or active subsector) falls under Monthly or Annual Three Killings.
* **Five Yellow (*Wu Huang*):** Rejection of dates where the active sector or sitting direction contains the Monthly or Annual Five Yellow Star, unless strictly conducting non-invasive Metal remediation.
* **Sitting Mountain Direct Clash:** Rejection if the Day Branch forms a direct 180° Earthly Branch clash with the specific animal sign of the Property Sitting Mountain (24 Mountains subsector):
  * *North 2 (Rat)* ↔ Reject Horse (*Wu*) Day
  * *Northeast 1 (Ox)* ↔ Reject Goat (*Wei*) Day
  * *Northeast 3 (Tiger)* ↔ Reject Monkey (*Shen*) Day
  * *East 2 (Rabbit)* ↔ Reject Rooster (*You*) Day
  * *Southeast 1 (Dragon)* ↔ Reject Dog (*Xu*) Day
  * *Southeast 3 (Snake)* ↔ Reject Pig (*Hai*) Day
  * *South 2 (Horse)* ↔ Reject Rat (*Zi*) Day
  * *Southwest 1 (Goat)* ↔ Reject Ox (*Chou*) Day
  * *Southwest 3 (Monkey)* ↔ Reject Tiger (*Yin*) Day
  * *West 2 (Rooster)* ↔ Reject Rabbit (*Mao*) Day
  * *Northwest 1 (Dog)* ↔ Reject Dragon (*Chen*) Day
  * *Northwest 3 (Pig)* ↔ Reject Snake (*Si*) Day

### 2.2 Personal & Zodiac Clashes
* **Year Breaker (*Sui Po* / Personal Clash):** The Day Branch must not form a direct 180° opposition with any primary client's or key occupant's Birth Year Branch (Zodiac Sign).
* *Note on Parallel Alignments (*Fu Yin*):* Same-branch arrivals (e.g., Ox day for an Ox client) are parallel alignments, not clashes, and do not trigger an automatic rejection unless combined with severe negative stars.

### 2.3 Universal Temporal & Macro Red Flags
* **Yang Gong Disaster Days (*Yang Gong Ji Ri*):** Universal annual taboo days.
* **Month Breakers (*Yue Po*):** Days where the Day Branch directly clashes with the Month Branch of the current solar term.
* **Astronomical Anomalies:** Days falling on Solar or Lunar Eclipses.
* **Prohibited Day Officers:**
  * **Destruction (*Po*) Day:** Prohibited for launches, activations, or new beginnings.
  * **Close (*Bi*) Day:** Prohibited for opening, launching, or commencing structural alterations.

## 3. Layer 2: Elemental & Event Alignment Rules

### 3.1 Elemental Match Requirements
* **Metal Placement / Remediation:**
  * Day Na Yin or Branch element must be **Earth** (to produce Metal) or **Metal** (to reinforce Metal).
  * Strictly avoid Fire-dominant Days or Hours, which clash with Metal and stimulate negative Earth Qi.
* **Fire Activation / Energy Boost:**
  * Day Na Yin or Branch element must be **Wood** (to produce Fire) or **Fire** (to reinforce Fire).
  * Avoid Water-heavy Days or Hours that extinguish activation energy.
  * **Rule of Non-Contradiction:** Never combine active Fire and Water elements in the same sector on the same day.

## 4. Layer 3: Day Officer & Energy Grading Hierarchy

Surviving dates are categorized according to their 12 Day Officers (*Jian Chu Shi Er Shen*) and supporting energy indicators:

### Priority 1: Primary "Good Days" (Core Candidates for Tier 1 & Tier 2)
* **Success (*Cheng*):** Optimal for all positive activations and maximizing outcome quality.
* **Open (*Kai*):** Optimal for starting, initiating, opening, and releasing energy.
* **Stable (*Ping*):** Preferred for long-term consistency and steady progress.
* **Initiate (*Jian*):** High vitality; suitable for launching, breaking ground, and initiating works.
* **Remove (*Chu*):** Optimal for clearing negative Qi, resetting spaces, or commencing remediation.

### Priority 2: Secondary / Conditional Days (Restricted to Tier 3 Backup)
* **Establish (*Ding*), Full (*Man*), Receive (*Zhi*), Balance (*Ping*):** Neutral or highly specialized conditional energy. 
* *Constraint:* Secondary days cannot be assigned to Tier 1 or Tier 2. They serve exclusively as Tier 3 backup dates if primary dates are unavailable and no hard afflictions exist.

## 5. Layer 4: Special Overrides & Mitigation

### 5.1 Noble Overrides (Great Sun & Great Moon)
* **Great Sun (*Tai Yang*) & Great Moon (*Tai Yin*) Safe Windows:**
  * If a property sitting carries minor background annual/monthly afflictions (e.g., Annual Three Killings on the sitting direction), the date may remain usable **IF** it coincides with a verified Great Sun or Great Moon noble alignment for that mountain.
  * *Condition:* Personal clashes (*Sui Po*) and direct physical drilling in Five Yellow sectors are strictly excluded from noble overrides.
  * *Audit Output Requirement:* Any date utilizing this override must include a formal **Client Disclaimer Note**.
